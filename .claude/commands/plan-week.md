Plan the coming week (Monday to Sunday, starting from the next Monday unless today is Monday).

1. Read the last 7 daily notes, `Agent/Learned.md`, `Agent/Durations.md`, `Roadmap/Roadmap.md`, `Agent/Calendar-Sources.md` and `Tasks/Inbox.md`.
2. Give me a short look back: completion rate, where estimates were off, patterns worth saving. Save real patterns to `Agent/Learned.md`.
3. Check the roadmap: what must move forward this week to stay on track? Flag milestones at risk.
4. Propose a week outline in chat: per day, fixed events, the 1-3 main tasks, habits, and the load level (light/normal/heavy). Respect the limits in `Agent/Preferences.md` and keep a light day.
5. Don't write daily plans yet. Once I approve the outline, create each `Daily/<date>.md` and fill its plan block following CLAUDE.md.
