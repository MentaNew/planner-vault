Run the onboarding interview for my planning agent.

Rules:
- Ask 2-3 questions at a time, never a long form. Wait for my answers.
- After each round, write what I said to the right file immediately (`Agent/Profile.md`, `Agent/Preferences.md`, `Agent/Calendar-Sources.md`, `Roadmap/Roadmap.md`) and say in one line what you saved. Log each change in `Agent/Changelog.md`.
- Replace placeholder values in Preferences with my real answers. Don't leave defaults I didn't confirm.

Cover, in this order:
1. Typical day: wake and sleep times, when I focus best, when I crash, meals, commute.
2. Classes and fixed commitments: course names, days and times, recurring meetings, anything that can never move.
3. Roadmap and goals: what I'm working toward this term, key deadlines, what "a good week" looks like.
4. Habits: which, how long (target and minimum), how often, preferred time.
5. Limits: max deep-work hours, longest session, how much of the day to keep free, light days.
6. How I plan today: what tools or notes I use, what usually goes wrong with my plans.

Finish by creating today's daily note from `Templates/Daily.md` if missing and proposing a first plan for tomorrow, following the planning rules in CLAUDE.md. Tell me what you're least sure about so I can correct it.
